Multi-touch strategy

How people buy

  • First touch: Facebook or Instagram ad
  • Second touch: Instagram organic or retargeting
  • Third touch: email sequence or direct website visit
  • Conversion: module purchase or quiz completion leading to module
  • Upsell: existing buyers emailed directly (not retargeted via ads)
  • Long-term: full academy, then subscription
